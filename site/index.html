Desktop app

Hide the window. Keep the timer running.

Open SittingTime from the system tray on Windows or the menu bar on macOS.

  • 01Tray-first by design. Closing the window hides it; the timer keeps running until you choose Quit.
  • 02Deadline-based timekeeping. The app owns the countdown, so sleep, a hidden window, or a restart won't turn elapsed time into guesswork.
  • 03Useful history, on your device. Review sitting, standing, rest, break counts, and longest sits by day or week. Set a daily standing goal if you want one.
  • 04No account or cloud sync. Timer state, settings, and statistics stay in local app storage.
